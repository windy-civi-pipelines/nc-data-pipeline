3 "PART II-A. ESTABLISH WORKGROUP TO EXAMINE STREAMLINING
4 REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS FOR PHYSICAL STRUCTURES OF LICENSED
5 CHILDCARE FACILITIES
6 SECTION 2A.1. The Division of Child Development and Early Education (the
7 Division) shall establish the Licensed Childcare Licensure Workgroup to examine streamlining
8 regulatory requirements related to the physical structures of licensed childcare facilities. The
9 workgroup shall consist of at least one representative from each of the following:
10 (1) The Division of Child Development and Early Education.
11 (2) The Office of the State Fire Marshal.
12 (3) The Department of Insurance.
13 (4) The North Carolina Building Code Council.
14 (5) The Division of Public Health.
15 (6) The League of Municipalities.
16 (7) The North Carolina Association of County Commissioners.
17 (8) N.C. Licensed Child Care Association.
18 (9) The North Carolina Child Care Commission.
19 (10) Other representatives deemed necessary by the Division.
20 The Division shall appoint two individuals to serve as co-chairs of the workgroup.
21 Each entity above shall choose the individual or individuals to serve on the workgroup
22 in a number to be determined by the Division.
23 SECTION 2A.2. The work group shall develop findings and recommendations
24 related to at least the following:
25 (1) Streamlining the regulatory requirements related to the physical structures of
26 licensed childcare facilities, including building codes, fire codes, and
27 sanitation codes.
28 (2) Resolving conflicts between various code requirements for licensed childcare
29 facilities.
30 SECTION 2A.3. The Division of Child Development and Early Education shall
31 report the findings and recommendations of the work group to the Joint Legislative Oversight
32 Committee on Health and Human Services, the Joint Legislative Oversight Committee on
